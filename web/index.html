About this demo and its limits

The same local CPU embedding model and TF-IDF baseline power sample and imported collections. Search compares a question with passages; link and overlap tools compare passages between articles. Full source text remains available for editorial review.

Cosine similarity measures mathematical closeness between text representations. It is not an accuracy percentage or a probability of relevance; the two methods' scores are not directly comparable.

Application resource limits: 250,000 title/body characters and 100 articles per collection, 20,000 body characters per article, 800 model passages and 40 selected pages per website import. These are application safeguards, not limits imposed by MiniLM: they bound memory, tokenisation, embedding work and processing time for multiple visitors in a small demo. Admission checks remain authoritative; no text is silently shortened to fit.

Recommendations are experimental suggestions, not instructions to modify a website. Development evaluation is historical, synthetic and separate from your collection.
